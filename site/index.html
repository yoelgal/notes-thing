Press N to add your own note. It’s timed from the first keystroke, so it lands beside what was being said, even if you finish typing later.

Free. Transcribes on your Mac. macOS 14 or newer. Or brew install --cask yoelgal/tap/notes-thing, or download the app.

A whole session, start to finish.

Nothing leaves your Mac

NVIDIA’s Parakeet model transcribes on your Mac. It downloads once (about 650 MB). No account, no upload.

Safe if your laptop dies

Notes hit the disk the moment you save them, and the audio records in a crash-safe format.

Your keys

⌃⌥P records and pauses. ⌃⌥N adds a note. Change either one in Settings.

Plain Markdown

Each lecture is a folder in ~/Sessions. Its name is copied for you, ready to paste into your AI assistant.

Made by Yoel Gal. Updates itself from GitHub releases. Interface adapted from Hex. Transcription by FluidAudio.
